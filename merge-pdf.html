    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I reorder the PDF files before merging?

    Yes. After adding your PDFs, you can drag and drop items in the list or use the up/down arrow buttons to set the exact merge order.

    Is there a page count or file count limit?

    There is no artificial page limit or daily quota. Because processing runs in browser memory, we recommend keeping individual files within 50 MB for smooth performance.

    Can I customize the merged PDF filename?

    Yes. You can enter any output filename in the options panel before clicking Merge PDFs (defaults to merged.pdf).

    Are my PDFs uploaded during merging?

    No. Merging happens 100% locally in your browser, so your documents stay on your device.

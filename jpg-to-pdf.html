    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does it support PNG or other image formats besides JPG?

    Yes. You can add .jpg, .jpeg, .png, and .webp images, and mix different formats in the same PDF.

    Can I adjust the PDF page size, orientation, and margins?

    Yes. You can choose A4, Letter, or "Fit to each image", set orientation (Auto, Portrait, or Landscape), and pick None (0 mm), Small (10 mm), or Normal (20 mm) margins.

    Can I reorder or rotate images before creating the PDF?

    Yes. After uploading, you can drag and drop images or use the up/down buttons to reorder them, and click the "Rotate 90°" button on any image.

    Are my photos uploaded to any server?

    No. Image reading, rotation, and PDF generation run entirely inside your browser, keeping your photos private on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it compress a PDF down to 100KB?

Yes, you can pick 100 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, or a custom target in KB (minimum 20 KB). If lossless optimization alone is still above your target, the tool progressively downsamples pages as JPEG images to reach it—though very large multi-page PDFs may stay above 100 KB to remain readable.

Will compressing my PDF reduce image or text quality?

Lossless structural optimization runs first with zero quality loss. If the file still exceeds your target size, pages are re-encoded as compressed JPEG images—the smaller the target size you choose, the lower the image quality and resolution.

What happens if my PDF is already smaller or heavily compressed?

If the compressed result is not smaller than the original file, the tool keeps and outputs the smaller version so your file never gets larger.

Is PDF compression done locally?

Yes, it is completely local. All compression takes place in your browser with no server uploads and no sign-up.
